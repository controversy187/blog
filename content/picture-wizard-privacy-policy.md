**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

This policy explains what information **Picture Wizard**, the Android app by Brett Fitzgerald ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email [brettgfitzgerald@gmail.com](mailto:brettgfitzgerald@gmail.com).

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information**

- If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account identifier, provided by Google Sign-In.
- If you sign in with email and password: your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
- An account ID that we assign, your credit balance, and the dates you created your account and last signed in.

**Content you create**

- The text prompts you enter for images and videos.
- Reference photos you choose from your gallery or take with your camera. They are sent to our image-generation provider for that one request and are not stored by us.
- The images and videos generated for you, with their prompts, settings and timestamps (your generation history).
- Images you mark as favorites.

**Purchases**

- When you buy credits, Google Play processes the payment. We receive and store the product you bought, the Google Play order ID, a purchase token used to confirm the purchase, and the date. We never receive your payment card details.
- A history of credit changes on your account (starter credits, purchases, generation charges and refunds).

**Content reports**

- If you report an image or video, we store the reasons you chose, any notes you add, your account ID and email address, your device model, language and app version, and the prompt and link of the reported item.

**Usage, diagnostics and device information**

- App usage events and diagnostics collected by Google Analytics for Firebase, tied to a random app-instance identifier. For some generation-error events, our server uses a one-way hash of your account ID instead.
- Device and app-integrity signals from Firebase App Check and the Google Play Integrity API, used to confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app.
- Server logs of requests to our backend, which can include your account ID.

## 2. How we use information

- To sign you in and keep your account secure.
- To generate images and videos from your prompts and show them in your gallery.
- To manage your credit balance and process purchases, including preventing a purchase from being redeemed twice.
- To review content reports and enforce our content rules.
- To understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve features.
- To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.

## 3. How your images and videos are stored

Images and videos you generate are stored in Google Cloud Storage at long, random web addresses so the app can display them and you can share them. **Anyone who has an item's address can view it**, but the addresses are not listed or searchable. If you share an image or video, the people you share it with can view it and share it further. When you delete your account, the files are deleted and their addresses stop working.

## 4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising. We share information only with these service providers, which process it on our behalf to run the app:

- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud**: sign-in (Firebase Authentication), database (Cloud Firestore), file storage (Cloud Storage), backend functions, App Check and logging.
- **Google Gemini API**: receives your prompts and any reference photos to generate images and videos.
- **Google Play**: billing for credit purchases, and the Play Integrity API.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: app usage statistics and diagnostics.
- **Discord**: when you sign up, buy credits or submit a content report, we send ourselves a private notification containing your name, email address and account ID. These notifications are only visible to us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the app or others.

## 5. Storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be located outside your country. Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our services. We limit access to your data, protect our backend with Firebase security rules and App Check, and protect our API keys. No security system is perfect, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. How long we keep information

- **Account data, content you create, favorites, credit history and reports:** until you delete your account.
- **Purchase records:** kept after account deletion, no longer linked to you, so a purchase cannot be redeemed twice and for refund and accounting purposes.
- **Server logs:** deleted automatically within 30 days.
- **Analytics data:** deleted automatically at the end of the retention period set in Google Analytics.

## 7. Your choices and account deletion

- You can view your images and videos in the app's gallery, and hide images you report.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time. Follow the steps at [brettgfitzgerald.com/picture-wizard-account-deletion](/picture-wizard-account-deletion/), or in the app open **Account & help** on the Generate screen and tap **Request account deletion**. We confirm the request with you and complete it within 30 days. That page lists exactly what is deleted and what is kept.
- Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
- To ask about, correct or get a copy of your information, email [brettgfitzgerald@gmail.com](mailto:brettgfitzgerald@gmail.com).

## 8. Children's privacy

Picture Wizard is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
